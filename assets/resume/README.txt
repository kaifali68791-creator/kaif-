RESUME — HOW TO ADD YOUR PDF
===========================

1. Copy your resume PDF into this folder, for example:
       kaif-resume.pdf

2. Open  index.html  and find this line (inside the nav and the contact section):

       <a class="btn btn-primary" href="#" data-placeholder download>Download Resume</a>

   Change it to:

       <a class="btn btn-primary" href="assets/resume/kaif-resume.pdf" download>Download Resume</a>

   (There are two such buttons — the nav button and the contact button.
    Update both, or search for:  data-placeholder download)

3. Done. The button will now download your PDF.
